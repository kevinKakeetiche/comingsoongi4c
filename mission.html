Who we are

A Community Data Intelligence & Systems Strengthening Hub

Making community needs visible for coordinated interventions.

Why GI4C exists

GI4C exists because communities cannot be effectively supported when their realities remain invisible. Across communities, people face complex and interconnected challenges including health risks, economic vulnerabilities, environmental pressures, humanitarian needs, and limited access to essential services.

Yet the information required to understand who is affected, where vulnerabilities are concentrated, why they persist, and what responses are most appropriate is often fragmented, delayed, or incomplete. This limits timely, evidence-informed decisions and effective targeting of resources and interventions.

GI4C continuously engages with communities, generates and integrates data from multiple sources, and transforms it into actionable intelligence that makes community needs, vulnerabilities, and opportunities visible to governments, donors, development partners, communities, private-sector actors and other stakeholders.

We combine community intelligence, research, health, GIS, monitoring and evaluation, and data systems to generate evidence for better planning, prioritization, resource allocation and coordinated decision-making.

Our role

We bring communities, government institutions, development partners, donors, private-sector actors and technical organizations together around shared priorities.

We connect evidence with institutions, resources, expertise and solutions, while strengthening local institutions, information systems and technical capacity for sustainable solutions.

Mission

Our Mission

To make community needs, vulnerabilities, and opportunities visible through continuous community intelligence, and to work with governments, communities, donors, development partners, and other stakeholders to translate evidence into better decisions, targeted resource allocation, and coordinated interventions that strengthen systems and drive sustainable impact.

Community intelligence→Evidence→Better decisions→Targeted resources→Coordinated interventions→Stronger systems→Sustainable impact
