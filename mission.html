ABOUT GI4C

A Community Intelligence & Systems Strengthening Hub

Connecting Community Intelligence to Decisions, Resources & Action

Global Impact for Communities (GI4C) is a non-profit organization and Community Intelligence & Systems Strengthening Hub. We combine technology, data, localized research and community knowledge in our work.

Our Mission

To leverage technology, data, and localized research to strengthen public systems and empower communities by translating real-time intelligence into informed decisions and coordinated action that advance healthy people, resilient systems, and sustainable communities.

Our Vision

A world where every community is visible, heard and empowered to shape the decisions, resources and actions that affect its future, supported by stronger systems and sustainable solutions.

Governance & Accountability

GI4C is committed to responsible, transparent and accountable stewardship of the people, resources, information and trust entrusted to us. Our governance approach promotes clear roles and responsibilities, ethical decision-making, responsible resource management, appropriate internal controls, transparency, responsible data practices and accountability for results.

We seek to ensure that decisions are guided by our mission, organizational values, evidence and the interests and dignity of the communities we serve. We are committed to strengthening our systems as the organization grows, learning from experience, documenting results and being transparent about both our achievements and areas where improvement is needed.

Our purpose

Why GI4C Exists

Communities experience complex and interconnected challenges, including health risks, economic vulnerability, humanitarian needs, environmental pressures and limited access to essential services. Yet the realities experienced by communities are not always sufficiently reflected in planning, decision-making, resource allocation and interventions.

Information may be fragmented, delayed or incomplete. Community knowledge may remain disconnected from formal evidence. Resources may not always reach the areas of greatest need, while organizations and institutions may lack the systems and capacities required to respond effectively and sustain solutions.

GI4C was created to help bridge these gaps. We listen to communities, assess their needs and priorities, generate and integrate data and local knowledge, and translate this information into actionable intelligence. We then work with communities, governments, civil society, development partners, donors and other stakeholders to mobilize resources, implement appropriate responses, strengthen systems and create conditions for sustainable change.

Our core pathway

Our Value Proposition

Local knowledge, evidence and implementation in one approach

GI4C brings community knowledge and research together with technical expertise, partnerships and implementation capacity. This helps communities and partners develop responses that fit local priorities and strengthens the organizations and systems needed to sustain them.

Our distinctive contribution is working across these capabilities rather than stopping at data collection or a short-term intervention.

Our beliefs

What We Believe

🌍

Development is interconnected

Health, livelihoods, food security, education, environmental sustainability, social well-being and resilient systems do not exist in isolation. A challenge in one area can affect another. Environmental degradation can affect livelihoods and food security. Economic vulnerability can affect access to health services. Weak systems can limit the ability of communities and institutions to respond to shocks.

We therefore seek to understand communities holistically, recognize the connections between their needs and opportunities, and support responses that address both immediate priorities and the systems that influence longer-term outcomes.

GI4C approach

Our Approach

From listening to lasting impact

Across all our areas of work, GI4C follows a community-centered approach that connects listening, evidence, action and systems strengthening. We do not stop at identifying needs. We work with communities and partners to understand priorities, mobilize people and resources, implement practical responses, strengthen local capacities and systems, learn from experience, and sustain or scale what works.

LISTEN→ASSESS→GENERATE INTELLIGENCE→IDENTIFY PRIORITIES→MOBILIZE→ACT→STRENGTHEN→LEARN→SUSTAIN & SCALE
  1. 01

    Listen

    We listen to communities, people and local actors to understand lived realities, priorities, concerns and aspirations.

  2. 02

    Assess

    We assess needs, vulnerabilities, risks, opportunities, existing resources, capacities and gaps.

  3. 03

    Generate Intelligence

    We combine community knowledge, data, research, technology and local evidence to generate actionable intelligence.

  4. 04

    Identify Priorities

    We work with communities and stakeholders to determine what matters most, who needs support, where action is needed and what response is appropriate.

  5. 05

    Mobilize

    We mobilize community participation, local resources, knowledge, expertise, partnerships and external resources needed to respond.

  6. 06

    Act

    We turn identified priorities into action. Depending on the context, we assist communities, implement interventions, provide technical support, facilitate services, mobilize resources or support partners to respond.

  7. 07

    Strengthen

    We strengthen people, organizations, institutions, services, systems and local capacities so that solutions can be sustained.

  8. 08

    Learn

    We monitor results, document experience, listen again and use evidence and feedback to improve what we do.

  9. 09

    Sustain & Scale

    We strengthen ownership, consolidate what works and support replication or scale where appropriate.

Cross-Cutting Approach

Youth-Led Innovation & Investment

From Community Ideas to Sustainable Solutions

GI4C works with young people not only as participants and community members, but also as innovators, entrepreneurs, researchers, creators and problem-solvers. Across our areas of work, we seek to identify promising youth-led ideas that respond to real community needs and help create pathways for those ideas to develop into practical, sustainable solutions.

Our approach combines community intelligence with mentorship, technical assistance, capacity strengthening, partnerships, resource mobilisation and catalytic investment. We support young innovators to understand the problem they are addressing, validate their ideas, develop and test solutions, learn from implementation and pursue pathways towards sustainability and scale.

This approach reflects a simple principle: communities should not only receive solutions; they should have the opportunity and capacity to create them. GI4C therefore seeks to help close the gap between ideas and implementation, potential and opportunity, and innovation and investment.

Discover

Identify promising ideas, talents and community-driven solutions.

Develop

Provide mentorship, technical assistance, skills and opportunities to test and refine ideas.

Invest

Connect promising solutions to catalytic funding, equipment, partnerships and other resources.

Sustain & Scale

Help viable solutions strengthen their models, measure results and connect with markets, institutions and further investment.

Our role

From intelligence to action and systems change

GI4C is a listener, intelligence generator, implementer, systems-strengthening partner and connector of people, resources and solutions.

Depending on the context and needs identified, we may:

  • Directly assist communities and vulnerable populations
  • Implement community-based programmes and interventions
  • Generate research, data and actionable intelligence
  • Provide technical assistance and expertise
  • Mobilize community participation, resources and partnerships
  • Strengthen organizations, institutions, services and information systems
  • Convene stakeholders around shared priorities
  • Connect identified needs with appropriate resources, expertise and solutions
  • Learn and adapt our approaches based on evidence and community feedback

Our role may differ from one context to another, but our purpose remains consistent: to help turn community realities and intelligence into informed decisions, practical action and stronger systems.

Values & principles

Our Values & Principles

GI4C applies two complementary frameworks that guide both who we are as an organization and how we work with communities.

C.I.R.C.L.E.

Our Organizational Values

C Community-Centred
We place community realities, priorities, dignity and lived experiences at the centre of our work.
I Integrity
We act honestly, transparently and responsibly, protecting trust, information and resources.
R Responsibility
We take ownership of our commitments, decisions, actions and results.
C Collaboration
We work across communities, government, civil society, development partners, academia and the private sector.
L Learning & Evidence
We continuously learn, generate reliable evidence and use intelligence to improve decisions and programmes.
E Equity & Inclusion
We seek to identify, include and support underserved populations and promote fair access to opportunities, resources and participation.
R.E.C.I.P.E.

Our Community Intervention Principles

R Responsibility
We act responsibly toward communities and the resources, information and trust entrusted to us.
E Empathy
We seek to understand people's experiences, perspectives, challenges and aspirations.
C Compassion
We ensure that evidence translates into humane, respectful and meaningful responses.
I Integrity
We engage communities honestly, protect confidentiality and maintain transparency.
P Purpose
We ensure every intervention has a clear objective and responds to an identified need.

We protect dignity, rights, privacy, safety, equity and informed participation.
Our Commitment

Accountable to people and communities

We are committed to working with communities and partners with integrity, accountability, transparency and respect for human dignity. We seek to protect the information entrusted to us, use resources responsibly, listen to community voices, learn from evidence and remain accountable for the results of our work.

We believe sustainable impact requires local ownership, meaningful participation, responsible use of data, strong partnerships and systems capable of sustaining solutions beyond individual projects. We seek not only to respond to today's needs, but to help build the capacity, systems and conditions that enable communities to thrive tomorrow.

Our Journey

Building for lasting impact

  1. November 2023

    The Idea

    The concept for GI4C emerged from experience working across humanitarian work, public health, data, information systems, research, programme implementation and systems strengthening, and from observing the gaps between community realities, evidence, decisions, resources and action.

  2. 8 June 2024

    Formal Establishment

    GI4C was formally established in Cameroon following the acknowledgement of its Declaration of Association.

  3. 2024-2026

    Building the Foundation

    GI4C began developing and testing initiatives across community intelligence, digital systems, health, humanitarian assistance, environmental sustainability, research and systems strengthening.

  4. Today

    Building for Impact

    GI4C is developing as a Community Intelligence & Systems Strengthening Hub, connecting community realities with evidence, decisions, resources and action.
