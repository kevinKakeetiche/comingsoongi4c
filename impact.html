Results in our programmes

Food & Dignity

In 2025, GI4C conducted three humanitarian assistance activities across Koupa Matapit, Ntamkene and Machatoum, reaching 126 people with food and clothing support.

View project details →

Restoring Community Forests, Biodiversity & Environmental Resilience

Implementation period: 2023–2028.

GI4C is supporting community-led reforestation in Machatoum, Massangam, Cameroon, to restore tree cover, protect biodiversity and contribute to a more resilient environment for present and future generations.

Partnership and growth

Ready to build on delivery

GI4C brings established governance procedures, community-based implementation experience and project coordination across humanitarian assistance and environmental restoration. These foundations position GI4C to work with communities, institutions and funders on larger collaborative programmes and to scale delivery responsibly.

Our approach connects community priorities with planning, implementation, coordination and documented results. We welcome partners who want to develop practical, accountable responses to community needs.
