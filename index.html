Who we are

A Community Data Intelligence & Systems Strengthening Non-Profit Organization

GI4C is a non-profit organisation dedicated to strengthening communities, institutions, and development systems through data intelligence, rigorous evidence, and operational innovation. As a Community Data Intelligence and Systems Strengthening Hub, we transform community data into actionable intelligence that helps governments, development partners, communities, and the private sector make better decisions, strengthen systems, and create sustainable impact.

We believe many of today's development, humanitarian, and public health challenges persist not because solutions do not exist, but because communities and decision-makers often lack timely, reliable, and actionable intelligence. By combining community intelligence, research, informatics, GIS, monitoring and evaluation, and systems strengthening, we generate evidence that informs action and delivers lasting results.

Our approach goes beyond implementing projects. We generate evidence before action, strengthen institutions, deliver integrated solutions across multiple development sectors, and build local capacity so that communities and systems continue to thrive long after projects end.

GI4C in action: strengthening health systems and livelihoods in the communities we serve.

Vision

A future where every community is visible, every decision is guided by evidence, and every investment delivers measurable, equitable, and sustainable impact.

Mission

GI4C's mission is to generate actionable intelligence, strengthen integrated community systems, and implement evidence-driven interventions that improve health, socio-economic resilience, environmental sustainability, and governance while informing decisions that help shape policies and create sustainable development outcomes both at local and national level.

Motto: Intelligence for Action & Systems for Impact

Slogan: Healthy People, Resilient and Sustainable Communities

Our Capacity

GI4C brings together multidisciplinary expertise, innovative technologies, and community-driven intelligence to help governments, development partners, and communities solve complex development challenges.

What we do

Our Work

GI4C is a Community Data Intelligence and Systems Strengthening organisation that transforms community data into actionable intelligence, strengthens systems, and delivers sustainable solutions for healthier, more resilient, and thriving communities.

Our name reflects our mission. “Global” represents our integrated, multi-sector approach that recognises communities are shaped by interconnected systems. “Impact” reflects our commitment to creating meaningful, measurable, and lasting change. “For Communities” places people at the centre of everything we do, ensuring that our work responds to real needs and delivers solutions where they are needed most.

Through an integrated, data-driven approach, we work across four interconnected strategic pillars to generate actionable intelligence, strengthen institutions, and improve lives.

We believe lasting development depends on strong health systems, inclusive socio-economic opportunities, resilient environments, and evidence-informed decision-making.

Partnership Opportunities

Partnership Opportunities

GI4C believes sustainable development is achieved through strong partnerships. We actively collaborate with governments, development partners, donors, academic institutions, civil society organisations, communities, and the private sector to co-design, resource, and implement innovative, evidence-driven solutions that create lasting impact.

We welcome partnerships in the areas of:

  • Technical Partnerships to collaborate on programme design, technical assistance, research, innovation, capacity strengthening, and knowledge exchange.
  • Funding & Resource Mobilisation to support the implementation and scale-up of strategic programmes through grants, sponsorships, investments, and co-financing.
  • Research & Academic Collaboration to partner on operational research, implementation science, surveys, evaluations, publications, and evidence generation.
  • Data, Digital & Innovation Partnerships to collaborate on GIS, digital health, artificial intelligence, data analytics, dashboards, interoperability, and community intelligence platforms.
  • Community Implementation Partnerships to work with GI4C to implement programmes that strengthen health systems, improve livelihoods, enhance climate resilience, and empower communities.
  • Institutional Partnerships to collaborate to strengthen organisational systems, governance, policy development, leadership, monitoring and evaluation, and institutional capacity.

Each partnership opportunity should include:

  • Partnership Overview
  • Objectives
  • Areas of Collaboration
  • Expected Contributions
  • Expected Benefits
  • Current Needs
  • Geographic Focus
  • Contact Partnership Team

Careers

There are many ways to make an impact. Whether you're seeking a career, consultancy assignment, internship, fellowship, or volunteer opportunity, GI4C offers a place to learn, to contribute your expertise, grow professionally, and help strengthen communities through intelligence, innovation, and systems thinking.

GI4C brings together professionals, researchers, innovators, consultants, and volunteers who believe that sustainable development begins with stronger systems and better decisions. If you're passionate about using your expertise to create measurable impact, we'd love to work with you.

Our Culture

At GI4C, we value curiosity, collaboration, integrity, innovation, accountability, and continuous learning. We foster an inclusive environment where diverse perspectives are welcomed and every team member contributes to strengthening communities through evidence-driven action.
