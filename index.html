Ayiagnigni Njoya Thomas Hugues Yannick (MPH, MBA) is a Public Health and Global Development Specialist with more than ten years of experience leading donor-funded programmes, strengthening health systems, advancing digital transformation, and building data-driven solutions across Cameroon. Among his notable achievements, Thomas led the design and national expansion of Cameroon's HIV Recency Surveillance Programme from 2 pilot regions to nationwide coverage, contributing to its institutionalisation within the national HIV response. Driven by a conviction that communities cannot be effectively developed if they remain invisible, Thomas founded GI4C to transform how community needs are understood and addressed. Under his leadership, GI4C is building a model where data is not merely collected, but transformed into actionable intelligence that empowers communities, informs decision-makers, and drives lasting impact.

Thomas believes sustainable development begins with listening to communities, strengthening institutions, and enabling governments and development partners to make better decisions through high-quality evidence, innovation, and collaboration. His leadership is guided by integrity, accountability, local ownership, systems thinking, and a commitment to ensuring that no community remains unseen, unheard, or underserved.

Who we are

A Community Data Intelligence & Systems Strengthening Non-Profit Organization

GI4C exists because communities cannot be effectively supported when their realities remain invisible.

Across communities, people face complex and interconnected challenges including; health risks, economic vulnerabilities, environmental pressures, humanitarian needs, and limited access to essential services. Yet the information required to understand who is affected, where vulnerabilities are concentrated, why they persist, and what responses are most appropriate is often fragmented, delayed, or incomplete. This limits the ability of governments, development partners, communities, and other stakeholders to make timely, evidence-informed decisions and effectively target resources and interventions.

Global Impact for Communities (GI4C) is a non-profit organization established to address this gap. As a Community Data Intelligence & Systems Strengthening Hub, GI4C continuously engages with communities, generates and integrates data from multiple sources, and transforms it into actionable intelligence that makes community needs, vulnerabilities, and opportunities visible to governments, donors, development partners, communities, private-sector actors, and other stakeholders.

We combine community intelligence, research, health, GIS, monitoring and evaluation, and data systems to generate evidence that supports better planning, prioritization, resource allocation, and coordinated decision-making.

Our role, however, extends beyond generating and disseminating evidence.

GI4C works across the ecosystem to bring communities, government institutions, development partners, donors, private-sector actors, and technical organizations together around shared priorities. We connect evidence with the institutions, resources, expertise, and solutions required to respond to identified needs and support coordinated interventions. At the same time, we strengthen local institutions, information systems, and technical capacity, to promote sustainable solutions that can endure beyond individual projects and funding cycles.

Our ambition is to make community needs visible, transform intelligence into coordinated action, connect the right resources and capabilities to the priorities that matter most, and contribute to stronger, more resilient communities and systems for sustainable development.

What we do

Our Thematic Area of Intervention

GI4C is a Community Data Intelligence and Systems Strengthening organisation that transforms community data into actionable intelligence, strengthens systems, and delivers sustainable solutions for healthier, more resilient, and thriving communities.

Our name reflects our mission. “Global” represents our integrated, multi-sector approach that recognises communities are shaped by interconnected systems. “Impact” reflects our commitment to creating meaningful, measurable, and lasting change. “For Communities” places people at the centre of everything we do, ensuring that our work responds to real needs and delivers solutions where they are needed most.

Through an integrated, data-driven approach, we work across four interconnected strategic pillars to generate actionable intelligence, strengthen institutions, and improve lives.

We believe lasting development depends on strong health systems, inclusive socio-economic opportunities, resilient environments, and evidence-informed decision-making.

Partnership Opportunities

Partnership Opportunities

GI4C believes sustainable development is achieved through strong partnerships. We actively collaborate with governments, development partners, donors, academic institutions, civil society organisations, communities, and the private sector to co-design, resource, and implement innovative, evidence-driven solutions that create lasting impact.

We welcome partnerships in the areas of:

  • Technical Partnerships to collaborate on programme design, technical assistance, research, innovation, capacity strengthening, and knowledge exchange.
  • Funding & Resource Mobilisation to support the implementation and scale-up of strategic programmes through grants, sponsorships, investments, and co-financing.
  • Research & Academic Collaboration to partner on operational research, implementation science, surveys, evaluations, publications, and evidence generation.
  • Data, Digital & Innovation Partnerships to collaborate on GIS, digital health, artificial intelligence, data analytics, dashboards, interoperability, and community intelligence platforms.
  • Community Implementation Partnerships to work with GI4C to implement programmes that strengthen health systems, improve livelihoods, enhance climate resilience, and empower communities.
  • Institutional Partnerships to collaborate to strengthen organisational systems, governance, policy development, leadership, monitoring and evaluation, and institutional capacity.

Each partnership opportunity should include:

  • Partnership Overview
  • Objectives
  • Areas of Collaboration
  • Expected Contributions
  • Expected Benefits
