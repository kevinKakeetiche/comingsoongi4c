Who we are

A data-driven NGO built for impact

Global Impact for Communities (GI4C) is a non-profit organization and Community Data Intelligence & Systems Strengthening Hub. We make community needs, vulnerabilities and opportunities visible and connect evidence with institutions, resources, expertise and solutions for coordinated action.

Current initiative

Operation 20K Trees

Restoring ecosystems and strengthening communities in Machatoum, Cameroon.

2,000trees planted
20,000target
18,000remaining
2026–2032duration

GI4C is implementing a community-based reforestation and agroforestry approach, combining planting, community engagement, site management, maintenance and long-term monitoring. A GIS/GPS-enabled tree registry will progressively document planting locations, species, dates, survival and growth.
