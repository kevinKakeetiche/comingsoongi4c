Food & Dignity

Supporting vulnerable community members through food assistance and human connection.

3Communities

Locations: Koupa Matapit, Ntamkene and Machatoum, Cameroon. Status: activities completed. Reach and activity figures are reported by GI4C.

Community context

Understanding the community need

Across communities, some individuals face difficulties meeting basic household needs while also experiencing loneliness, loss or limited social support.

GI4C’s Food & Dignity initiative was designed to provide practical assistance to vulnerable community members, including widows caring for children and older people or parents facing hardship without adequate support.

The initiative recognized that responding to vulnerability involves more than material assistance. It also requires treating people with dignity, listening to their experiences and creating opportunities for meaningful human connection.

Implementation

From assistance to human connection

In 2025, GI4C organized three support activities in Koupa Matapit, Ntamkene and Machatoum. The activities reached 126 people across the three locations, providing essential food items and clothing.

Alongside material assistance, the activities created opportunities for conversation, shared stories, music, dancing and encouragement. These interactions reflected GI4C’s commitment to recognizing the dignity and humanity of people experiencing difficult circumstances.

The initiative sought to create an environment in which community members could share time together, feel acknowledged and experience moments of connection beyond their immediate hardships.

Learning and collaboration

Lessons and future opportunities

The initiative demonstrates GI4C’s commitment to community responses that recognize both material needs and human dignity.

Future activities could benefit from partnerships that support essential supplies, community-based assistance, outreach to vulnerable households and consistent approaches to identifying and responding to local needs.

GI4C welcomes collaboration with donors, businesses, community organizations and development partners interested in dignity-centred humanitarian assistance and community well-being.

Our commitment: Community assistance should recognize people not merely through their vulnerabilities, but through their dignity, experiences and inherent human value.
