Operation 20K Trees: Restoring Community Forests, Livelihoods & Biodiversity

Community Reforestation for Environmental Restoration, Climate Resilience & Sustainable Livelihoods

📍 Machatoum Community, Massangam, West Region📅 2023–2028🏷️ Climate, Environment & Resilience

❤️ The Challenge

Residents of Machatoum reported that prolonged tree cutting had significantly changed their local environment, including the loss of vegetation, shade and trees traditionally used for food and other community needs. The loss of ancestral, culturally significant and landmark trees has also affected the community's natural and cultural landscape. Residents described increasing exposure to heat and sunlight, soil degradation and erosion, greater vulnerability of agricultural land and crops, and the displacement of wildlife from areas where trees had traditionally provided habitat and shelter.

💚 Our Response

Following community consultations and a needs assessment, GI4C supported the community to prioritize reforestation as a practical response. GI4C launched Operation 20K Trees, targeting the planting and establishment of 20,000 trees to contribute to environmental restoration, biodiversity, community resilience and future livelihood opportunities.

🤝 Our Role

GI4C serves as the initiator, funder and implementer, leading community engagement, needs assessment, planning, tree planting, documentation and ongoing monitoring.

🎯 Results & Progress

  • 2,300 trees planted to date
  • 4 tree species established: Ayous (1,200), Sapelli (500), Bubinga (300), Iroko (300)
  • Community participation in environmental restoration strengthened
  • Initiative recognized by local forestry authorities, the Sub-Divisional Officer and Massangam Municipality
  • Long-term objective to restore vegetation, biodiversity and ecological functions while creating future livelihood opportunities

Food & Dignity: A Lifeline for the Forgotten

Support for Vulnerable Families Widows, Elderly, and Those Left Behind

📍 Koupa Matapit, Ntamkene & Machatoum, West Region📅 2025 (Recurring)🏷️ Humanitarian Assistance

💔 The Heartbreaking Reality

In the quiet corners of Koupa Matapit, Ntamkene, and Machatoum communities, there are faces etched with silent suffering. Widows who lost their husbands too soon, now left alone to raise children with no means to feed them. Elderly men and women who once contributed to their communities, now abandoned by family members, unable to care for themselves. Many go days without proper meals. Their greatest pain is not just hungerit's the crushing weight of loneliness, the humiliation of asking for help, and the despair of feeling forgotten by the world.

These are not statistics. These are mothers, fathers, grandparentspeople who lived full lives before circumstance stripped away their dignity. They face not only food insecurity but profound social isolation and emotional hardship that few can truly understand.

💚 Our Response: More Than Food

GI4C provided essential food items and clothing while creating time and space to listen, share stories, offer comforting words, laughter and encouragement. Through conversation, music and dance, we sought to remind people facing loss, loneliness and hardship that they are seen, valued and not forgotten. For a few hours, they were not defined by their vulnerability, they were simply people sharing food, stories, love and human connection.

🤝 Our Role

GI4C serves as initiator, funder, organizer, and implementer working directly with communities and local authorities to reach those most in need.

🎯 The Impact: Restoring Dignity

  • 126 vulnerable people reached
  • 3 activities conducted in 2025 (in 3 sessions)
  • Food and clothing assistance provided to those most in need
  • Community members received opportunities for social connection, listening, and emotional encouragement
  • Local leadership engaged in identifying and reaching the most vulnerable

📊 Capacity Building
Planned initiative

CBO Institutional Data & Reporting Systems Strengthening

Helping Community-Based Organizations Build Sustainable Digital Systems

📍 Proposed focus: Centre, Littoral & West Regions📅 2026–2027🏷️ Systems & Capacity

💔 The Challenge

Many community-based organizations (CBOs) across Cameroon lack reliable, organization-owned systems for managing data, monitoring results and reporting. Some depend on project-specific digital platforms or tools created and controlled by external partners; when those projects end, organizations may lose access to their data, tools, workflows and institutional knowledge. Others have no dedicated data or reporting systems and rely on paper forms, manual registers or basic counts to track activities, while some reporting may be based on incomplete records or informal recollection. These practices make it difficult to maintain accurate records, analyze results over time, demonstrate achievements, produce quality reports, support fundraising and use evidence for organizational decision-making.

💚 Our Response

GI4C has developed digital data and reporting systems ready for deployment with participating CBOs. The proposed initiative will configure and implement these systems in partnership with up to 10 organizations across the Centre, Littoral and West Regions, adapting them to organizational needs and supporting staff to use and maintain them. Implementation is subject to partnership development and resource availability.

🎯 Proposed Results

  • Up to 10 CBOs supported to establish or strengthen organization-owned data systems
  • Improved M&E, data-quality and reporting practices
  • Staff trained to operate and use the systems
  • Lessons documented to guide potential future expansion
