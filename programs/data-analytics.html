Data Intelligence & Digital Systems

GI4C uses community-generated data, digital systems, GIS, research and analytics to understand who needs support, where needs are greatest, what is changing and where action is needed.

What we do

We design and strengthen community data systems, digital data collection tools, GIS and mapping solutions, dashboards, analytical products, research and evidence systems that transform fragmented information into reliable, localized intelligence.

Why it matters

Good development decisions require more than data. They require relevant, timely and localized intelligence that can help communities, governments, organizations and development partners understand priorities, identify gaps, target resources and coordinate action.

How it contributes to development

Our work helps make community needs and opportunities more visible, strengthen evidence-informed planning, improve resource targeting, reduce information gaps and support more effective, equitable and context-responsive development action. We connect community intelligence to decisions, resources and action, helping stakeholders identify where support is most needed, prioritize and target resources, coordinate interventions, strengthen systems and adapt responses to changing realities. The result is more informed, equitable, coordinated and context-responsive development action that contributes to healthier people, stronger systems and more sustainable communities.
