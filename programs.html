Current initiative

Operation 20K Trees: Restoring Ecosystems, Strengthening Communities

Project Initiator & Initial Funder: GI4C Board of Directors · Implementer: Global Impact for Communities (GI4C) · GI4C Role: Technical Implementer and Project Coordination · Strategic Theme: Environmental Sustainability & Community Resilience · Duration: 2026–2032

20,000trees target
2,000already planted
18,000remaining
MachatoumCameroon

GI4C identified concerns around tree loss, land degradation, declining biodiversity and increasing environmental vulnerability. The initiative aims to restore degraded areas, protect soils, support biodiversity and strengthen community stewardship.

Key species initially include Ayous, Sapelli, Iroko, Bubinga and other appropriate native species. GI4C is progressively developing a GIS/GPS-enabled tree registry to document planting locations, species, planting dates, survival and growth.
