Operation 20K Trees: Restoring Community Forests, Livelihoods & Biodiversity

Community Reforestation for Environmental Restoration, Climate Resilience & Sustainable Livelihoods

📍 Machatoum Community, Massangam, West Region📅 2023–2028🏷️ Climate, Environment & Resilience

❤️ The Challenge

Residents of Machatoum reported that prolonged tree cutting had significantly changed their local environment, including the loss of vegetation, shade and trees traditionally used for food and other community needs. The loss of ancestral, culturally significant and landmark trees has also affected the community's natural and cultural landscape. Residents described increasing exposure to heat and sunlight, soil degradation and erosion, greater vulnerability of agricultural land and crops, and the displacement of wildlife from areas where trees had traditionally provided habitat and shelter.

💚 Our Response

Following community consultations and a needs assessment, GI4C supported the community to prioritize reforestation as a practical response. GI4C launched Operation 20K Trees, targeting the planting and establishment of 20,000 trees to contribute to environmental restoration, biodiversity, community resilience and future livelihood opportunities.

🤝 Our Role

GI4C serves as the initiator, funder and implementer, leading community engagement, needs assessment, planning, tree planting, documentation and ongoing monitoring.

🎯 Results & Progress

  • 2,300 trees planted to date
  • 4 tree species established: Ayous (1,200), Sapelli (500), Bubinga (300), Iroko (300)
  • Community participation in environmental restoration strengthened
  • Initiative recognized by local forestry authorities, the Sub-Divisional Officer and Massangam Municipality
  • Long-term objective to restore vegetation, biodiversity and ecological functions while creating future livelihood opportunities

Food & Dignity: Humanitarian Support for Vulnerable Community Members

Food and clothing assistance in three West Region communities

📍 Koupa Matapit, Ntamkene & Machatoum, West Region📅 2025🏷️ Humanitarian Assistance

Community Context

Community engagement in Koupa Matapit, Ntamkene and Machatoum identified people experiencing heightened vulnerability, including widows, older people and households facing food insecurity. These locally identified needs informed GI4C’s humanitarian assistance activities.

Response and Delivery

In 2025, GI4C conducted three humanitarian assistance activities, providing food and clothing support to community members. Activities were organized with local community leaders and municipal authorities, with opportunities for participants to connect and engage.

GI4C’s Role

GI4C financed, coordinated and implemented the activities in collaboration with local community leaders and municipal authorities.

Reported Outputs

  • 126 people reached across three communities
  • Three humanitarian assistance activities conducted in 2025
  • Food and clothing support provided
  • Local community leaders and municipal authorities engaged in delivery

CBO Institutional Data & Reporting Systems Strengthening

Helping Community-Based Organizations Build Sustainable Digital Systems

📍 Proposed focus: Centre, Littoral & West Regions📅 2026–2027🏷️ Systems & Capacity

💔 The Challenge

Many community-based organizations (CBOs) across Cameroon lack reliable, organization-owned systems for managing data, monitoring results and reporting. Some depend on project-specific digital platforms or tools created and controlled by external partners; when those projects end, organizations may lose access to their data, tools, workflows and institutional knowledge. Others have no dedicated data or reporting systems and rely on paper forms, manual registers or basic counts to track activities, while some reporting may be based on incomplete records or informal recollection. These practices make it difficult to maintain accurate records, analyze results over time, demonstrate achievements, produce quality reports, support fundraising and use evidence for organizational decision-making.

💚 Our Response

GI4C has developed digital data and reporting systems ready for deployment with participating CBOs. The proposed initiative will configure and implement these systems in partnership with up to 10 organizations across the Centre, Littoral and West Regions, adapting them to organizational needs and supporting staff to use and maintain them. Implementation is subject to partnership development and resource availability.

🎯 Proposed Results

  • Up to 10 CBOs supported to establish or strengthen organization-owned data systems
  • Improved M&E, data-quality and reporting practices
  • Staff trained to operate and use the systems
  • Lessons documented to guide potential future expansion

GI4C Youth Innovation & Investment Initiative

From Community Ideas to Sustainable Solutions

📍 Community-wide, national and partner-based📅 Developing / Upcoming🏷️ Youth, Innovation, Investment & Systems Strengthening

💡 The Challenge

Across communities, young people are developing ideas that could address challenges in health, agriculture, livelihoods, technology, climate resilience, environmental sustainability and other areas of community development. Yet many promising ideas do not progress beyond the concept stage because young innovators lack access to mentorship, technical expertise, networks, early-stage resources and opportunities to test their solutions. At the same time, communities continue to face challenges for which locally developed solutions could offer practical and sustainable responses.

🌍 Our Response

GI4C is developing a youth innovation and investment initiative designed to identify, develop and connect promising youth-led solutions with the support and resources needed to move from ideas to implementation. Through mentorship, technical assistance, innovation challenges, strategic partnerships, resource mobilisation and, subject to available funding, catalytic investment, the initiative will support selected young innovators to develop and test solutions responding to real community needs.

🤝 Our Approach

Discover → Develop → Validate → Invest → Sustain → Scale

GI4C will focus not simply on distributing funding, but on helping innovators build solutions that demonstrate relevance, feasibility, measurable impact and pathways towards sustainability. GI4C seeks to mobilise and facilitate access to catalytic investment and other resources for promising youth-led solutions.

🎯 Priority areas

  • Health and community well-being
  • Agriculture and livelihoods
  • Climate and environmental sustainability
  • Digital and technological innovation
  • Social and economic development

🧭 Support Stages

Discover → Develop → Validate → Sustain & Scale

🤝 Core Support

Mentorship • Technical Assistance • Catalytic Investment

✅ What we aim to achieve

The initiative aims to help young innovators transform promising ideas into practical solutions, enterprises, community initiatives and scalable models that contribute to stronger communities and sustainable economic opportunities.
